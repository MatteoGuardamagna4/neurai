# Explanation

Let's go through break-even revenue together. You can compute it in euros of sales rather than in units, and when a business sells lots of different things that is the only workable way. The key tool is the contribution margin ratio: the slice of every euro of sales that remains, after the variable cost of that sale has been paid, to cover the fixed costs.

To get the ratio, take the contribution margin per unit, meaning the selling price minus the variable cost, and divide it by the selling price. A ratio of 0.7 tells you that 70 cents of each euro of revenue goes towards the fixed costs and, once those are covered, towards profit, while 30 cents pays for the product or service itself. Because the ratio is a share of the price and never of the cost, it always sits between zero and one.

Divide the fixed costs by that ratio and you get the sales level at which the business breaks even:

Break-even revenue = Fixed costs / Contribution margin ratio

Worked example. A bakery's fixed costs are EUR 96,000 a year. A cake sells for EUR 40, and its ingredients, packaging and delivery cost EUR 15. Contribution margin = 40 - 15 = EUR 25, which makes the contribution margin ratio 25 / 40 = 0.625. Break-even revenue = 96,000 / 0.625 = EUR 153,600 a year.

The mistake people make most is dividing by the wrong ratio. At the bakery, ingredients, packaging and delivery eat up 15 / 40 = 0.375 of each euro of sales; divide the fixed costs by that and you get 96,000 / 0.375 = EUR 256,000, far more sales than the bakery actually needs. The two ratios always sum to one, so name each before you divide: the variable cost ratio is what a sale uses up, the contribution margin ratio is what a sale leaves. Keep that difference in mind as I walk you through the calculation below, because picking the ratio is where people slip most.

# Problem

A subscription business has fixed costs of EUR 252,000 per year. Each subscription sells for EUR 60 and carries variable servicing costs of EUR 24. What annual sales revenue does it need to break even? There is no need to work it out yourself. If you answer wrongly, I will show you the full calculation and the answer straight away, one step at a time.

# Worked solution

Step 1: find the contribution margin per subscription: 60 - 24 = EUR 36. Step 2: make it a ratio by dividing by the price: 36 / 60 = 0.6, so 60 cents of each euro of revenue remains to cover fixed costs. Step 3: divide the fixed costs by that ratio: 252,000 / 0.6 = EUR 420,000 of revenue a year. Dividing by the variable cost ratio of 0.4 would give EUR 630,000 instead, because that ratio measures the share of revenue used up, not the share left over. The ratio is what makes it work: it turns a margin per unit into a share of revenue, and that is why the answer comes out in euros.

Use these three steps for the rest of the unit: find the contribution margin per unit, divide it by the price to get the ratio, then divide the fixed costs by that ratio. If a question also asks for a profit, add the profit to the fixed costs before dividing. You don't need to work it out again; just follow the steps as shown.
