# Explanation

Let us examine break-even revenue together. It may be calculated in euros of sales rather than in units, and for a business selling a wide range of products this is the only practicable approach. The relevant instrument is the contribution margin ratio: the proportion of each euro of sales that remains, once the variable cost of that sale has been met, to cover the fixed costs.

The ratio is obtained by dividing the contribution margin per unit, that is, the selling price less the variable cost, by the selling price. A ratio of 0.7 signifies that 70 cents of every euro of revenue is available for the fixed costs and, beyond them, for profit, whereas 30 cents covers the goods or the service itself. Since the ratio is a proportion of the price and never of the cost, it invariably lies between zero and one.

Dividing the fixed costs by this ratio yields the level of sales at which the business breaks even:

Break-even revenue = Fixed costs / Contribution margin ratio

Worked example. A bakery incurs fixed costs of EUR 96,000 per year. A cake is sold for EUR 40 and costs EUR 15 in ingredients, packaging and delivery. Contribution margin = 40 - 15 = EUR 25, and the contribution margin ratio is therefore 25 / 40 = 0.625. Break-even revenue = 96,000 / 0.625 = EUR 153,600 per year.

The most frequent error is division by the wrong ratio. In the bakery example, ingredients, packaging and delivery absorb 15 / 40 = 0.375 of every euro of sales; dividing the fixed costs by this ratio gives 96,000 / 0.375 = EUR 256,000, a considerable overstatement of the sales required. As the two ratios always sum to one, each should be identified before dividing: the variable cost ratio is what a sale consumes, the contribution margin ratio what it leaves. Bear this distinction in mind as I present the calculation below, since the choice of ratio is the step most frequently mistaken.

# Problem

A subscription business has fixed costs of EUR 252,000 per year. Each subscription sells for EUR 60 and carries variable servicing costs of EUR 24. What annual sales revenue does it need to break even? It is not necessary to solve this yourself. Should your answer be incorrect, I shall present the complete calculation and the answer immediately, step by step.

# Worked solution

Step 1: calculate the contribution margin per subscription: 60 - 24 = EUR 36. Step 2: convert it into a ratio by dividing by the price: 36 / 60 = 0.6, so 60 cents of every euro of revenue remains to cover fixed costs. Step 3: divide the fixed costs by that ratio: 252,000 / 0.6 = EUR 420,000 of annual revenue. Division by the variable cost ratio of 0.4 would instead yield EUR 630,000, since that ratio measures the share of revenue consumed rather than the share remaining. The ratio is what makes the method function: it converts a margin measured per unit into a proportion of revenue, which is why the result is expressed in euros.

Apply these three steps for the remainder of the unit: calculate the contribution margin per unit, divide it by the price to obtain the ratio, then divide the fixed costs by that ratio. Where a question also specifies a profit, add the profit to the fixed costs before dividing. No further derivation is required; apply the steps exactly as presented.
