# Explanation

Break-even analysis may be conducted in euros of revenue rather than in units, and for a business selling a wide range of products this is the only practicable approach. The relevant instrument is the contribution margin ratio: the proportion of each euro of sales that remains, once the variable cost of that sale has been met, to cover the fixed costs.

The ratio is obtained by dividing the contribution margin per unit, that is, the selling price less the variable cost, by the selling price. A ratio of 0.7 signifies that 70 cents of every euro of revenue is available for the fixed costs and, beyond them, for profit, whereas 30 cents covers the goods or the service itself. Since the ratio is a proportion of the price and never of the cost, it invariably lies between zero and one.

Dividing the fixed costs by this ratio yields the level of sales at which the business breaks even:

Break-even revenue = Fixed costs / Contribution margin ratio

Worked example. A bakery incurs fixed costs of EUR 96,000 per year. A cake is sold for EUR 40 and costs EUR 15 in ingredients, packaging and delivery. Contribution margin = 40 - 15 = EUR 25, and the contribution margin ratio is therefore 25 / 40 = 0.625. Break-even revenue = 96,000 / 0.625 = EUR 153,600 per year.

The most frequent error is division by the wrong ratio. In the bakery example, ingredients, packaging and delivery absorb 15 / 40 = 0.375 of every euro of sales; dividing the fixed costs by this ratio gives 96,000 / 0.375 = EUR 256,000, a considerable overstatement of the sales required. As the two ratios always sum to one, each should be identified before dividing: the variable cost ratio is what a sale consumes, the contribution margin ratio what it leaves. The sequence is: subtract, divide by the price, then divide the fixed costs by the result. The same ratio resolves target-profit questions once the required profit has been added to the fixed costs.

# Problem

A subscription business has fixed costs of EUR 252,000 per year. Each subscription sells for EUR 60 and carries variable servicing costs of EUR 24. What annual sales revenue does it need to break even? Solve it independently. Should your answer be incorrect, up to three hints will follow, one at a time, each followed by a further attempt.

# Hints

Hint 1: The question requires a revenue, not a number of units, so the divisor must be a ratio rather than an amount in euros. Identify the two euro amounts from which that ratio is constructed.

Hint 2: The contribution margin ratio equals the contribution margin per unit divided by the selling price. Calculate the margin per unit first, then express it as a proportion of the price.

Hint 3: Divide the fixed costs by the contribution margin ratio obtained. The quotient is the sales revenue at which the business breaks even.

# Worked solution

Step 1: calculate the contribution margin per subscription: 60 - 24 = EUR 36. Step 2: convert it into a ratio by dividing by the price: 36 / 60 = 0.6, so 60 cents of every euro of revenue remains to cover fixed costs. Step 3: divide the fixed costs by that ratio: 252,000 / 0.6 = EUR 420,000 of annual revenue. Division by the variable cost ratio of 0.4 would instead yield EUR 630,000, since that ratio measures the share of revenue consumed rather than the share remaining.
